Fallback LLM (optional but recommended)

When the primary provider returns 429 Too Many Requests (e.g. Groq's daily free-tier quota) or a 5xx outage, the extension will transparently retry on this fallback. Recommended setup: OpenRouter with a :free model. Get a free key at openrouter.ai/keys.

Paste your resume and the JD here, OR drop them into resume.txt (or resume.md) and job-description.txt (or jd.txt) at the extension root. Files are auto-synced on every extension reload, and the buttons below pull updates without a reload. Both filenames are gitignored.

Behavior

Auto-answer debounce: how long we wait after the speaker stops before asking the LLM. The "incomplete" wait kicks in only when the trailing text looks mid-thought (trailing comma, connector word like "and"/"to", etc.) - it gives the speaker more breathing room before we commit. Default 800 / 2000 ms.

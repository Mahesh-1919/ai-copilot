AdShield Lite
Idle
Filtering ad and tracker requests in the background. Four ways to open the floating stats panel (any one works, all are invisible during a tab- or window-share):
  • The small AS circle in the bottom-right of any tab
  • Pop out panel button above (this popup)
  • Ctrl+Shift+Y in any tab. If it doesn’t fire, click Configure shortcut — Chrome silently drops bindings that clash with other apps.
  • Right-click anywhere on the page → Show AdShield panel
Use Pause to suspend filtering on a noisy page, Resume to re-enable, End to stop the shield.
